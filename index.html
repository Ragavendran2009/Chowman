The House

Street-cart
heat, sit-down
service.

Chowman brings the tadka of Kolkata's Tiretti Bazaar Chinese stalls to a proper table — red lacquer booths, quick-fire woks, and a kitchen that doesn't hold back on the chilli oil.

The brand traces back to a single outlet in Kolkata in 2010, built by musician-turned-restaurateur Debaditya Chaudhury on the idea that Indo-Chinese food deserved a proper sit-down home rather than just a roadside stall. It's since grown into a chain across Kolkata, Bangalore, Delhi-NCR and Hyderabad — the Arekere outlet is the South Bangalore branch of that story.

Regulars come back for the manchow soup and the kolkata-style chilli chicken; first-timers usually leave asking for the fried rice recipe.
